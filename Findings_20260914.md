# Template Comparison Findings

Environment: staging
Path: service
Templates compared: default, mc-tiers-dobb, mc-tiers-nameb
Requested page sizes: 10 and 20
Searches compared: approximately 3,850 per pairing

## Template choice is not a differentiator

- All three templates returned the same volume: 2.6 identities per search across every pairing and both page sizes
- Result set overlap was 97.5 to 98.2 percent across all six pairwise comparisons
- At most 2 searches out of approximately 3,850 returned fewer identities under any template, in either direction
- Top hit disagreement was 0 to 2 searches per comparison

## The flagged top hit gap does not hold on the service path

- 2,983 of the 2,985 searches that returned anything shared the same top identity, or 99.9 percent
- The 77.5 percent headline figure is diluted by zero result searches, not by ranking disagreement

## Zero result searches are the material finding

- 863 of approximately 3,850 searches, or 22 percent, returned no identity under every template
- Concentrated in FIRST at 512 of 846 searches, or 60 percent, and BHUB at 343 of 778 searches, or 44 percent
- CRIS recorded 8 zero result searches; GLOBAL and UIPATH recorded none
- A reproducible criteria pattern returned zero under all three templates: any combination containing middle name plus country_of_birth, and the combination of first, last, dob, alien_nbr with country_of_citizenship and no country_of_birth
- That pattern covers roughly 420 searches, or about 11 percent of traffic, and is consistent with the known middle name pruning behavior. Direct confirmation is still required.

## Live traffic is identifier driven, not name driven

- receipt_nbr alone accounts for 39.9 percent of searches and alien_nbr alone accounts for 18 percent, or 58 percent combined
- Name only searches are negligible: first and last appeared 3 times, first, middle and last appeared once
- The tiered name boosting ladder is barely exercised by production traffic

## Consumers split into two distinct workloads

Broad search

- BHUB returned 5.2 to 6.0 identities per search
- FIRST returned 3.6 to 4.2 identities per search

Identifier lookup

- CRIS returned 1.1 identities per search, with exactly one identity on 885 of 956 searches
- UIPATH returned 1.1 identities per search, with exactly one identity on approximately 503 of 585 searches
- GLOBAL returned 1.0 identities per search, with exactly one identity on 671 of 684 searches

Identifier submission behavior

- Only FIRST submits more than one identifier, on 161 of 846 searches, or 19 percent
- GLOBAL is 100 percent alien_nbr
- UIPATH is 100 percent receipt_nbr
- CRIS is 99.5 percent receipt_nbr

## Implication

- Any of the three templates can proceed to staging on these results; template selection is not the lever
- Remaining value sits in the zero result criteria patterns and the planned DOB reindex, not in further tier tuning

## Caveat

- Search counts differ by one on a small number of file and template rows, for example first-a.csv at 420 versus 421 and uipath.csv at 585 versus 586, indicating minor call level variance in the run
