Medical necessity - how to read the file

Dave,

All six categories are scored 0 to 2, as scoped. The total adds three of
them - mobility, level of consciousness and medical monitoring - for a
documentation score of 0 to 6, where a 0 means nothing was documented.
Level of service, modifiers and isolation are scored and shown, but kept
out of the total because they land on every order regardless of what the
facility documented.


THE SIX CATEGORIES

   All six are scored 0 to 2:

      Score: mobility
      Score: LOC
      Score: monitoring
      Score: isolation
      Score: LOS
      Score: modifiers

   Each one, from what the model recorded for each answer:

      2   the answer supports necessity
      1   addressed but not sufficient on its own
      0   administrative, not covered, unclear, or nothing documented


THE TOTAL

   Score: documentation   mobility + LOC + monitoring, 0 to 6

   Three of the six are not in the total:

      Score: LOS          every order has a level of service, so this
                          is 1 or 2 on every trip, never 0
      Score: modifiers    every order has an origin and destination
                          code, so this is 1 or 2 on every trip
      Score: isolation    always 0, no form question asks about
                          isolation precautions

   Those three describe the trip rather than the documentation. Added
   in, they put a floor of 2 under every total, so a trip with nothing
   documented scored 2 or 3 out of 12.

   The three in the total have a real zero. A 0 means nothing was
   documented.

   Say the word if you want the 0 to 12 total of all six back as a
   second column.

   The three scored categories are colour shaded.


THE SCORE DOES NOT DECIDE THE DETERMINATION

   - The score and LLM: meets are both built from the same per-question
     findings.

   - The determination also applies the criteria weights, the exclusions,
     and the rules that require elements together. Bed confinement needs
     all three.

   - A trip can carry three "not sufficient alone" findings, score 3, and
     correctly not meet.

   - LLM: basis is the model's own line on what drove the determination.

   - Score vs determination flags the rows where the two conflict
     sharply: meets with nothing documented, or does not meet on a score
     of 4 or more. Those are the rows worth your time.


THE DETERMINATION

   LLM: meets                      yes or no. This is the answer.
   LLM: confidence                 High, Medium or Low. Not derived from
                                   the score.
   LLM: summary                    two sentences on the patient
   LLM: clarifying question        what we would ask the facility, blank
                                   when nothing is needed
   LLM: assessment per question    per answer: what the model recorded,
                                   the reason, the answer quoted, the
                                   citation. Look here when you disagree.
   AUDIT columns                   flag anything the model asserted that
                                   is not in the order


THE ORDER AS ENTERED

   Questions and answers    every question asked and what was answered
   Other form info          diagnosis, weight, Medicare question 10,
                            special needs
   Duplicate payloads       whether the order was submitted more than once


THE PATIENT CARE RECORD

   ePCR columns    what the crew documented
   Agreement       whether the order and the record reached the same
                   conclusion


YOUR COLUMNS

   SME: meets, SME: notes    blank, for your determination
   Flags                     failed calls, no matching record


FAILED CALLS

   A trip the model never assessed has blank scores, not zeros. The
   reason is in Flags.


LAYOUT

   Row 1 is the group banner and the scale. Row 2 is the headers. Every
   column filters.


DECISIONS FOR YOU

   - Scale. 0 to 6 is three categories at 0 to 2, so any score
     decomposes by eye. Say the word if you want 0 to 10.

   - Isolation. Should the order form ask about isolation precautions?
     The criteria recognise it; the form does not ask.

   - Category weights. The criteria file weights concepts at 0.3, 1.0 and
     2.0. A stretcher-only finding counts for more than a bariatric one.
     The score treats them equally. We can carry the weights through if
     you want the score to rank strength rather than coverage.
